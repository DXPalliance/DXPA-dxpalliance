What is The Discovery Exchange?

The Discovery Exchange (Tdex) brings together a community marketplace, opportunities for family property ownership, student discovery, and Human–AI collaboration. The idea is to help families turn everyday economic activity into a path toward ownership and opportunity while supporting the next generation of discovery.

What makes Tdex different?

Tdex connects activities that are usually separate: selling unused belongings, building toward property ownership, supporting student achievement, and giving ordinary human owners a meaningful voice in governance. Its governing principle is simple: one human owner, one vote.

What does “From the Garage to the Future” mean?

Items gathering dust in one family's garage may be useful to another family. When those items are sold through Discovery Market, the transaction can help both buyer and seller build toward participation in the broader Tdex ecosystem.

What is Discovery Market?

Discovery Market (DM) is the buying-and-selling marketplace within Tdex. Families can offer ordinary things they no longer need and use eligible Marketplace transactions to build toward property ownership through their DXPA Wallets.

What happens when an item sells?

Under the Marketplace's 50 / 25 / 25 structure, the sale proceeds are allocated as follows: • 50% to the seller in cash. • 25% to the seller's DXPA Wallet, designated toward property ownership. • 25% to the buyer's DXPA Wallet as cashback, also designated toward property ownership. This is the allocation model, not a promise that either wallet instantly becomes a property deed. Applicable transaction terms and disclosures explain the actual settlement process.

Can you give me a simple example?

For a qualifying $100 sale under the stated allocation formula, $50 is allocated to the seller as cash proceeds, $25 to the seller's DXPA Wallet, and $25 to the buyer's DXPA Wallet. Any applicable taxes, fees, returns, and other transaction requirements must be explained in the transaction's terms.

Why does the buyer receive a wallet allocation?

Tdex is designed so that a qualifying purchase can help both sides of a transaction participate. The seller receives cash and a wallet allocation; the buyer receives a wallet allocation toward the same longer-term property-ownership goal.

Can I sell ordinary household items?

Yes. The Marketplace is intended for everyday goods such as furniture, tools, bicycles, musical instruments, and other eligible items. Product, safety, shipping, and legal rules determine what may be listed.

Does a Marketplace purchase automatically make me a property owner?

No. A Marketplace transaction creates the wallet allocations described above. An actual property ownership interest exists only when the relevant eligibility, transaction, legal documentation, and property acquisition requirements have been completed.

What is DXPA?

DXPA stands for Discovery Exchange Parents Alliance. It is the part of Tdex focused on participating families and the property-ownership framework.

What is a DXPA Wallet?

A DXPA Wallet records eligible allocations designated toward the property-ownership program. A wallet balance and an issued ownership interest are different things.

What is a Nugget?

“Nugget” is Tdex's working name for a fractional ownership interest associated with a specific participating property. A Nugget is not an entire building, and it is not Discovery BigCoin. The precise legal rights of any interest are determined by the governing property documents.

Does owning a Nugget mean I own a fraction of a real property?

The intended DXPA model connects qualifying fractional interests to identifiable participating real estate. The legal form, ownership rights, transfer rules, and associated obligations must be stated in the property-specific documents before a purchase is completed.

Can participating properties generate rental income?

Yes. A participating rental property may generate income after operating expenses and applicable obligations. Rental income is never guaranteed: vacancies, repairs, financing costs, market conditions, and other factors can reduce or eliminate distributions.

How is net distributable rental income allocated?

Under the proposed program structure, after lawful property expenses, taxes, insurance, reserves, and other obligations have been handled, eligible net distributable rental income is allocated: • 70% to eligible participating property owners. • 30% to support the National K–12 Discovery Prize. The 70 / 30 model applies to net distributable rental income, not gross rent or every dollar paid by tenants.

Is a DXPA Wallet balance the same as rental income?

No. Wallet allocations are part of the path toward property participation. Rental income, when available, comes from an eligible property interest and is governed by that property's documents.

Can property interests be transferred or inherited?

The applicable property documents and law govern transfers, inheritance, eligibility, and any restrictions. No universal right to immediate resale or cash withdrawal should be assumed.

What is Discovery BigCoin?

Discovery BigCoin (DBC) is Tdex's separate fixed-supply digital participation-value system. It is distinct from the fractional property interests known as Nuggets and from balances held in a DXPA Wallet.

Is Discovery BigCoin the same as a Nugget?

No. A Nugget refers to an interest associated with a particular participating property. DBC belongs to a separate digital system. Holding DBC does not, by itself, establish ownership of a particular apartment building.

How does Discovery BigCoin connect to real property?

Tdex's intended economic architecture includes a pathway in which funding associated with Discovery BigCoin supports acquiring properties through auctions. Properties acquired under that pathway would enter the broader Tdex property ecosystem under their applicable ownership, accounting, and legal arrangements. The exact funding mechanism and rights associated with DBC must match the operative system documents and disclosures; DBC itself should not be described as a property deed. Discovery BigCoin funding pathway ↓ Property acquisitions at auction ↓ Participating real property in the Tdex ecosystem

Is Discovery BigCoin Bitcoin?

No. Discovery BigCoin is Tdex's separate system. It is not Bitcoin and should not be confused with Nuggets or DXPA Wallet allocations.

Does Discovery BigCoin guarantee profits or ownership?

No. A fixed supply does not guarantee a market value, profit, liquidity, or ownership rights in any specific property. Applicable terms will define its uses and restrictions.

What is the National K–12 Discovery Prize?

Inspired by the idea of recognizing remarkable achievement, the National K–12 Discovery Prize is Tdex's program for recognizing and rewarding young people for discovery, ingenuity, creativity, building, improving, investigating, solving problems, and helping others learn.

Does a student need expensive equipment to participate?

No. The program is designed to value the quality and usefulness of a student's discovery or work rather than the size of a family or school's budget. An excellent project made with ordinary materials deserves serious consideration.

How is the Discovery Prize connected to property ownership?

The intended 70 / 30 net rental distribution connects eligible participating properties to the Prize: 70% of net distributable rental income goes to eligible property owners and 30% supports the National K–12 Discovery Prize.

Is every student guaranteed an award?

No. Awards depend on program rules, eligibility, judging, available funds, and the merits of a submission. The purpose is to expand opportunity, not promise an award to everyone who applies.

Who owns and controls Tdex?

Tdex is organized around human ownership and equal human participation in governance. Under its foundational principle, one human owner receives one vote. Having more money or owning more property does not buy additional governing votes.

What does “one human owner, one vote” protect?

It is intended to prevent wealth from purchasing governing control. Financial participation and governing voting power are separate: larger holdings do not automatically confer extra votes, permanent board seats, or special political influence.

What is the National Mom & Pop Board?

The National Mom & Pop Board is the proposed rotating owner-governance body that gives ordinary owners a direct voice in oversight and decisions. Owner participation and selection procedures are designed to prevent control from becoming concentrated in a permanent few.

What is the Internal Arbitration Court?

The Internal Arbitration Court is a separate process for considering disputes and reviewing eligible matters under Tdex's governing rules. Independent owner juries are intended to prevent the people running operations from being the sole judges of their own conduct. Its powers, procedures, and any legally binding effects are determined by applicable documents and law.

What is the Owners Chat Room?

The Owners Chat Room is a dedicated space for eligible owners to exchange views, ask questions, and take part in community conversation. Conversation alone is not a formal vote, board action, or court ruling.

Can the rules ever change?

Any change must follow the applicable constitutional process, rather than being imposed simply because a wealthy participant or administrator wants it. The governing documents—not a summary FAQ—control the formal procedure.

What role does artificial intelligence play in Tdex?

Tdex uses separately defined AI roles to help investigate proposals, verify work, organize records, and answer public questions. AI assistance is not a substitute for lawful human authority or constitutional safeguards.

Who is Columbo?

Columbo is the internal investigator AI. Columbo examines evidence, tests assumptions, looks for problems, and proposes solutions. In everyday language, Columbo asks, “Does this make sense?”

Who is Pipito?

Pipito is the independent verification AI. Its role is to check whether proposed protected actions comply with the relevant rules and authorization process. Investigation and verification are deliberately separated; neither role should have unlimited authority acting alone.

Who is The Concierge?

The Concierge is the public-facing information AI. Visitors can ask questions about Tdex's programs, terminology, and website. The Concierge's informational role is separate from protected institutional approvals and private account authorizations.

What is DILOS?

DILOS is the internal operating system intended to record, route, track, verify, and preserve institutional work and decisions. It supports institutional continuity and accountability behind the scenes.

Does Tdex sell my personal information?

Tdex's stated policy is not to sell private household or personal data as an advertising product. Access to identity information, financial records, minors' data, and other sensitive records is governed by privacy, security, and applicable legal requirements. The published Privacy Policy should explain any necessary processing and disclosure in detail.

Are all financial records publicly visible?

No. Tdex's transparency goals must be balanced with privacy. Authorized oversight and auditing are different from exposing individual owners' private financial or identity information to the public.

How does the whole system fit together?

Here is the Mom & Pop version of the intended ecosystem: Something unused in your garage ↓ Sell it through Discovery Market (DM) ↓ 50% seller cash / 25% seller DXPA Wallet / 25% buyer DXPA Wallet ↓ Eligible wallet allocations build toward property participation ↓ Participating properties may generate net rental income ↓ 70% to eligible property owners / 30% to National K–12 Discovery Prize Alongside this path, Discovery BigCoin (DBC) is a separate digital participation-value system whose intended funding architecture includes property acquisitions through auctions. DBC and Nuggets are not the same asset.

Does the system guarantee that my family will become wealthy?

No. The goal is to create access to opportunity and transparent participation, not to promise particular earnings, property appreciation, returns, or financial outcomes.

Is Tdex only about money?

No. Economic participation is meant to support a larger mission: ordinary families having an opportunity to build ownership, children having opportunities to be recognized for discoveries, and human owners having an equal voice in the rules.

Where can I learn about my rights, eligibility, and fees?

Review the published program terms, property-specific documents, privacy information, and any required financial or legal disclosures for the program you want to use. The FAQ explains the concept; the operative terms determine actual rights and obligations.

Is there someone who can explain the system in simple language?

Yes. The Concierge is designed as the first stop for public questions and directions to the appropriate program information.
